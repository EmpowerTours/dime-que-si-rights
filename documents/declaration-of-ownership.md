## Declaration

I, **Earvin Gallardo**, declare the following in respect of the sound recording identified
above.

**1. Authorship.** I am the sole author of the lyrics, melody, topline and vocal arrangement
embodied in this recording, and the sole performer of the vocals. I wrote, performed and
recorded this work in Mexico in 2026. No other person contributed authorship to the
composition.

**2. Ownership.** I hold one hundred percent (100%) of the writer's share, one hundred percent
(100%) of the publisher's share, and one hundred percent (100%) of the master recording rights
in this work.

**3. Licensed instrumental.** The recording incorporates a licensed instrumental, *Mr Mustaco —
"BAILE FUNKY"*, produced by Ben Kamberi, professionally known as Your Loving Lounge. I
purchased a commercial licence for it on 28 April 2026 under invoice
`SND144787_0114547001777434753_1915869_150106823840722`. The producer is credited on this
release as "Produced by Your Loving Lounge."

**4. Distribution rights.** That licence expressly grants commercial distribution and
exploitation rights. Paragraph 4(b) on page 1 grants the right to:

> "…make the New Song available for sale in physical and/or digital form and sell Unlimited
> downloads/physical music products and are allowed Unlimited monetized audio streams,
> Unlimited monetized video streams…"

and to perform it:

> "…on the internet via third-party streaming services (Spotify, YouTube, iTunes Radio etc.)"

**5. Content matching.** The instrumental was licensed on a non-exclusive basis. Other artists
have recorded their own separate songs over the same backing track, and this recording may
therefore match those releases on content recognition systems. Each such recording is a
distinct work. I claim no right in any other artist's recording, and no other artist holds any
right in mine. Because the instrumental is non-exclusive, this release is not submitted to
content identification systems.

I declare that the statements above are true and accurate to the best of my knowledge.
